# DESIGN

## Concept

Warm, calm, content-first interface inspired by personal journals.

## Tone

* Not cute
* Not playful
* Not colorful
* Calm, warm, personal

## Colors

* Background: #F8F5F0
* Surface: #FFFFFF
* Primary: #7A5230
* Primary Soft: #EDE3D7
* Accent: #C89F7A
* Text Primary: #2C2C2C
* Text Secondary: #6F6F6F
* Border: #E7E2DA

## Typography

* Font: Pretendard, Inter, system-ui
* Clean and readable hierarchy

## Layout

* Max width: 1120px
* 8px spacing system
* Generous padding

## Components

### Card

* White surface
* Rounded (16px)
* Soft border

### Button

* Primary: warm brown
* No flashy animation

### Input

* Clear border
* Comfortable writing experience

## Rules

* Do not introduce new colors
* Do not add unnecessary animation
* Keep UI simple and consistent
